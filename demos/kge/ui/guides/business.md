# Business team

You confirm what each drafted term means. Your acceptance records that you have checked the work; a different steward certifies it before release. Your assigned permissions determine which work you may review and accept.

## 1. Open the draft

Sign in, choose Context from the app switcher, and open Onboard. Select the business area awaiting review.

## 2. Confirm the meaning

Read the terms, mappings, ontology, industry standards, data policies and documents. Check each definition, figure, restriction and proposed removal with the source owner. Read What changed before deciding whether the draft represents the intended meaning.

## 3. Accept the items you checked

Review every item in the unit, then choose Accept all only when you have confirmed them all. For an improvement proposal, review that proposal's items before accepting them. Do not accept work whose meaning is still unresolved; ask the knowledge engineer to correct it first.

Your decision is recorded in history. A different steward must certify accepted work. Acceptance does not publish a new graph version.
