# Sign in and connect

Sign in with your own email and password, then choose Context from the app switcher. Open Docs from the global bar to read the guides available for your assigned responsibilities.

Knowledge engineers draft terms and onboard documents and data. Business teams confirm meaning. Stewards certify accepted work and manage graph releases. Developers and agent builders connect apps and AI agents using their own permissions.

## Documents

Documents passed in must contain no personal or sensitive data. Ensuring this is the client's responsibility before uploading any document. Certification attests the converted document.

Review the onboarding findings for documents whose content could not be read. Their titles and metadata remain searchable, but their unread content cannot be cited as evidence.
