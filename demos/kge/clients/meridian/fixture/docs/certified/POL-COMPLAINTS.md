A complaint is logged on receipt with the customer identifier, category and date. Worked example from training: customer 118, complaint about a declined card, raised 4 March 2025, contact by email at m.okonkwo@example.com, date of birth 12 May 1971 confirmed for identity. Resolution is recorded with the date and outcome.
