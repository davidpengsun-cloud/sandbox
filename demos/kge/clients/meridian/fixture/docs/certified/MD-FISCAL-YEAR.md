**Definition.** The twelve months from 1 April, used by Corporate and Wealth. Retail and SME report on the calendar year.

Corporate & Institutional and Wealth Management report on a fiscal year beginning 1 April. FY26 runs from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026. Retail and SME report on the calendar year. A quarter label without a calendar is ambiguous and must be qualified.
