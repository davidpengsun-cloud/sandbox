Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Personal data of customers is Restricted by default. Restricted data may not be embedded, indexed or otherwise vectorised for retrieval by any assistant. Supervisory review tooling may retrieve Restricted material under a logged supervision purpose only.
