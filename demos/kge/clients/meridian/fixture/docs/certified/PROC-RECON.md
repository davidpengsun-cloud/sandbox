Each quarter Group Finance assembles enterprise revenue from the four unit figures. Retail and SME monthly figures are summed to the calendar quarter. Corporate and Wealth fiscal quarters are re-cut to the calendar quarter by month where available and by a stated allocation where not. Adjustments are documented. The result is the only enterprise revenue figure the bank publishes, and it is never a plain sum of the four certified columns.
